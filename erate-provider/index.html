USAC's rules, in USAC's words

This page states no rule of its own. Where a result touches a rule, these are the sentences it refers to, quoted from USAC's pages.

Invoice deadline extensions

  • USAC's Invoicing page: “Upon request, invoice filers are entitled to a one-time, 120-day deadline extension per FRN. The request must be made before, or within 15 days after, the original IDD.”
  • USAC's Invoice Deadline Extensions page: “If your IDD has passed but you are still within 15 days of the IDD, you can request a one-time invoice deadline extension by opening a Customer Service Case in EPC and listing the FRN(s) you want to extend.” And: “If you need an invoice deadline extension, but the deadline to request one has passed (i.e., it is more than 15 days after the IDD), you must first submit a waiver request to the Federal Communications Commission (FCC).”
  • For a request made in EPC's extension tool (the route while the IDD hasn't passed), the same page says, for service providers: “The requestor must be a full- or partial-rights user on the EPC profile for the Service Provider Identification Number (SPIN) featured on the FRNs.” And: “A service provider can file a single request for FRNs from multiple funding years and/or multiple BENs.” It doesn't say who may open the Customer Service Case after the deadline.

IDD is USAC's abbreviation for the invoice deadline date, the last date to invoice. This page counts the 15 days as calendar days; USAC's page doesn't say calendar or business days.

SPAC (FCC Form 473)

  • USAC's File the FCC Form 473 page: “Service providers must file their SPAC Form before any FCC Forms 472 (BEAR) or FCC Forms 474 (SPI) invoices can be processed and paid by USAC.” “USAC must have a SPAC on file for the relevant funding year in order to be able to disburse funds for an FRN for that funding year.” “Therefore, the SPAC Form should be filed no later than June 30 of the applicable funding year (i.e., June 30, 2025, for SPINs with FY 2024 FRNs) at issue to allow time for E-Rate participants to submit their requests for reimbursement (i.e., SPI and BEAR requests) for that funding year.”
  • The same page: “File this form in the E-Rate Productivity Center (EPC).” And: “View the status of your SPAC for a particular funding year in the Service Provider (SPIN) Download Tool or the Open Data FRN Status Tool FY2016 to verify that USAC has successfully processed your SPAC form.”

Quotes as read on USAC's pages on November 5, 2026. Information only, not E-Rate compliance advice: confirm the route, the dates and your EPC rights before you act.

How to read a result

Each field is USAC's, with USAC's own note for it from its data dictionary.

Committed
USAC: “The total amount for the Funding Request (FRN) after discount.” It is the most that can be invoiced on the FRN, not an amount owed.
Disbursed
USAC: “Total net amount disbursed to a service provider or applicant.” Not yet disbursed isn't the same as not yet invoiced: USAC's data shows payments, not invoices still in review, and a service that cost less than committed shows a gap too.
Invoicing mode
BEAR (FCC Form 472) or SPI (FCC Form 474). NOT SET is USAC's value “when no form has been received yet for the FY and therefore no invoicing mode has been set - only applicable for funding years before 2026”. This page reads NOT SET as no invoice received only for FY2025.
Last date to invoice
USAC: “Last date an Invoice (BEAR or SPI) can be received or postmarked”.
Extension requested
USAC: “Indicates whether a one-time, 120-day extension for invoice submission was requested on or before the original deadline.” So a request made after the deadline may not show here.
Invoicing ready
USAC: “Invoicing may begin for the FRN as there is an approved FCC Form 486.”
SPAC filed (on each FRN)
USAC: “Indicates if an FCC Form 473 Service Provider Annual Certification (SPAC) was filed for the Funding Year associated with the FCC Form 471.”
Form 473 years and status (USAC's SPIN data)
USAC: “Lists the funding years for which the service provider has filed an FCC Form 473.” And: “Indicates whether a service provider is labeled active or closed in the E-Rate program.”
Extension window open until
15 calendar days after a last date to invoice that has passed, shown when USAC's data shows no extension requested by the deadline and the FRN isn't fully disbursed.

Where the data comes from

  • FRNs: USAC Open Data, E-Rate Request for Discount on Services: FRN Status (dataset qdmp-ygft, Public Domain, updated daily). One query per check: the Current version of every FRN with status Funded in funding years 2025 and 2026 whose service provider number (USAC's field epc_organization_id) is the SPIN you enter, 18 named fields, no contact fields. A SPIN with more than 5,000 such FRNs takes one more request per 5,000.
  • SPAC years and status: USAC Open Data, E-Rate Service Provider Information (dataset xcy2-bdid). One query per check, three fields: spin, status and fcc_form_473_spac_filed.
  • "Data as of" is each dataset's last update in USAC's metadata, read once per visit.
  • Left out: FRNs that show $0 committed (nothing to invoice) and FRNs whose applicant's name suggests a health-care operator. A provider's name is shown only when it reads as a business or public body.
  • Westworks counts visits and checks on this page, never the SPIN.
