What it is, and what it isn't

Snow Ledger is a fast, cheap, checkable first answer: for counting pushes and inches, reconciling invoices, answering "did it snow on the 9th?", and finding the right records when a claim letter arrives. It is not what certified services sell. Those put a meteorologist's review or certification behind the totals, and cost more: one storm-report service publishes $149 per storm report and $1,750 for an unlimited season1; NOAA's certified copies are $180 per order2; forensic meteorologists quote per case. If your client, insurer or contract requires certified or meteorologist-reviewed totals, buy those instead. Snow Ledger tells you which station and dates to order certified copies of.

FAQ

Isn't this data free? Yes, all of it. We save you pulling three NOAA systems, lining up observation windows, throwing out observers who skipped a day, and grouping hours of airport reports into storms, and we do it again after every storm.
Why is your number lower than the airport's? Often it is. The airport is one gauge, sometimes 10 or more miles from your lot; the median of dozens of observers near you is a better estimate for your site. Both are in every row, with the full range.
Does it renew? No. Next fall we may email you once about next season. That's all.
What happens to my information? We use your email only for your ledger, your questions and refunds. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list.
