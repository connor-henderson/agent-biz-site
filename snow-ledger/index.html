What it is, and what it isn't

Snow Ledger is a fast, cheap, checkable first answer: for counting pushes and inches, reconciling invoices, answering "did it snow on the 9th?", and finding the right records when a claim letter arrives. It is not what certified services sell. Those put a meteorologist's review or certification behind the totals, and cost more: one storm-report service publishes $149 per storm report and $1,750 for an unlimited season1; NOAA's certified copies are from $180 per order2; forensic meteorologists quote per case. If your client, insurer or contract requires certified or meteorologist-reviewed totals, buy those instead. Snow Ledger tells you which station and dates to order certified copies of.

FAQ

Isn't this data free? Yes, all of it. We save you pulling three NOAA systems, lining up observation windows, throwing out observers who skipped a day, and grouping hours of airport reports into storms, and we do it again after every storm.
Which number do I bill on? That is up to your contract, and every row carries all three: the airport total, the observers' median and range, and NOAA's grid value. The observer median often runs below the airport: in the downtown Minneapolis sample the airport was higher on 9 of last winter's 10 events of 1 inch or more (40.0 inches against 32.8 over the 10). The airport is one gauge, sometimes 10 or more miles from your lot; the median shows what the observers nearer your lot measured.
What if a storm already hit my area this season? It's in your first ledger. Whichever day you buy, you get every event at your address since October 1, 2026, plus all of last winter, for the same price.
Does it renew? No. Next fall we may email you once about next season. That's all.
What happens to my information? We use your email only for your ledger, your questions and refunds. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list.
