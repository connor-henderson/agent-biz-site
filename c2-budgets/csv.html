What's in each row

rank, ben, entity_name, entity_type, city, state, budget_status
USAC's Billed Entity Number (BEN), name, type (School District, School, Library, Library System), city, and budget status (Forecast, Preliminary, Confirmed). Sorted by budget left, largest first.
full_time_students, library_sq_ft, child_entities
The inputs USAC bases the budget on, and how many schools or branches sit under the entity.
c2_budget_fy2026_2030, funded, pending, left, pct_left
USAC's five-year pre-discount C2 budget and its own funded, pending and available figures.
c2_discount_pct, c2_discount_from_fy, est_erate_share_of_left
The discount on the entity's latest funded or pending C2 funding request filed under its own BEN, the year it came from, and our estimate of what E-Rate would pay on the rest of the budget (left × discount). Blank when the entity hasn't filed a C2 request under its own BEN since FY2021.
prev_cycle_budget_fy2021_2025, prev_cycle_funded, prev_cycle_used_pct
Last cycle's budget and how much of it was funded: a quick read on whether the entity actually spends its C2 money.
c2_frns_fy2021_on, last_c2_frn_fy, c2_vendors_fy2021_on, latest_c2_contract_end
Funded or pending C2 funding requests (FRNs) filed under the BEN since FY2021, the latest year, the top 3 service providers by pre-discount amount, and the latest contract expiration date on those FRNs.
consultant_crn
The Consultant Registration Number USAC lists on the entity's budget record, if any. Look it up in USAC's data if you need the firm. We don't put names of people in the file.
flags
forecast_not_validated, requests_exceed_budget, large_single_school_forecast: the same caveats the free lookup shows.
f470_fy2026_count, f470_fy2027_count, latest_470_*, fy2027_c2_470_bidding_open
Certified Form 470s filed under the BEN for FY2026 and FY2027, the newest one's number, year, categories, certified date and allowable contract date, and yes when a FY2027 Form 470 that includes Category 2 still has its 28-day bidding window open on the day you download.
usac_data_pulled_utc
When your browser pulled the data from USAC.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Three USAC Open Data datasets, queried live from your browser: C2 Budget Tool Data FY2021+ (6brt-5pbv), Form 471 FRN Status (qdmp-ygft), and FCC Form 470 Tool Data (jt8s-3q52). USAC publishes all three under a Public Domain license. Westworks is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, USAC or the FCC.

Are there contact names or emails?

No. The CSV carries entity names, types, cities, BENs, budget figures, form numbers, and service providers' business names as USAC publishes them, and nothing about individual people. USAC states that email harvesting is not an authorized use of its open data. Find the right contact through the entity's own website or its Form 470.

Couldn't I build this myself?

Yes. Everything comes from public USAC data, and the code that builds it (c2csv.js) is open. The $49 pays for the joins: this cycle's budget, last cycle's use, the C2 discount, vendors and Form 470 status in one row per entity, refreshed every time you download. The download page doesn't check your payment with Stripe; it trusts the checkout link, so please be fair.

How fresh is it?

As fresh as USAC's data at the moment you download. When we checked on 2026-11-02, USAC's metadata showed all three datasets updated within the previous two days. You can rebuild the same state's CSV from your download link later.

What if it doesn't work?

If the CSV fails to generate for your state, or a figure in it doesn't match USAC's data as published that day, ask for a refund within 14 days from the link on the download page and we refund the full price. If USAC's service is down, try the same link later.

Who runs this?
