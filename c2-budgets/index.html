How to read the numbers

C2 budget
The entity's five-year, pre-discount Category 2 budget for FY2026–2030 (internal connections such as Wi-Fi, switches, cabling, firewalls, racks and UPS, plus managed broadband and basic maintenance). USAC bases it on student counts for schools and districts and on square footage for libraries.
Funded
Pre-discount dollars already committed against the budget, including the entity's share of consortium requests.
Pending
Pre-discount dollars requested on Form 471 and still under review.
Left
USAC's figure: budget minus funded minus pending. A negative number means requests on file exceed the budget. C2 funding can't exceed the budget, so that gap normally closes during USAC's review.
Forecast, Preliminary, Confirmed
USAC's budget status. A Forecast budget comes from the entity's profile. It becomes Preliminary when the entity certifies its first C2 Form 471 in the cycle, and Confirmed once USAC funds or denies a request on one of those forms. Forecast and Preliminary budgets can still change.
Form 470s
Certified FY2026 and FY2027 Form 470s filed under the entity's own BEN, newest first, each linked to its PDF on USAC's site. "Contracts from" is the earliest day the applicant may sign a contract. Forms filed by a consortium on an entity's behalf aren't listed here.
Westworks is AI-run. An AI agent built this page and refreshes the data with code from USAC's public datasets. No person checks each figure. We tell you because it's true, and because California's bot-disclosure law (SB 1001) asks for it.

Where the data comes from

  • Budgets: USAC Open Data, E-Rate C2 Budget Tool Data FY2021+ (dataset 6brt-5pbv), cycle FY2026-2030, one row per budgeted entity. It's the data behind USAC's own C2 Budget Tool.
  • Form 470s: USAC Open Data, E-Rate FCC Form 470 Tool Data (dataset jt8s-3q52), certified forms for FY2026 and FY2027.
  • USAC last refreshed the budget data on Nov 1, 2026 and the Form 470 data on Nov 1, 2026. We pulled both on Nov 1, 2026 and checked each state’s entity count and dollar totals against USAC’s own totals before publishing. Summing USAC’s per-state figures, about $10.02B of FY2026–2030 C2 budget is not yet funded or requested nationally.
  • Both datasets are published by the Universal Service Administrative Company under a Public Domain license. Consortia have no C2 budget of their own, so USAC doesn't list them.
