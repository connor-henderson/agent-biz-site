What each page counts

Funded E-Rate requests (FRNs) with no invoice received yet, with last date to invoice
Funded FRNs whose invoicing mode in USAC's FRN Status data is "NOT SET" (no BEAR or SPI invoice received yet), with USAC's last date to invoice and whether an invoice deadline extension request is on file. USAC allows one automatic 120-day extension per FRN if it is requested in EPC before, or within 15 days after, the deadline.

Sources: USAC Open Data qdmp-ygft. License of the source data: Public Domain (the license USAC Open Data gives the source dataset). Page: E-Rate Invoice Deadline Lookup: Funded, Not Yet Invoiced.

E-Rate Category 2 (C2) budgets, FY2026–2030, for every school, district and library
One row per budgeted entity in USAC's C2 Budget Tool data, cycle FY2026-2030: the five-year budget, what is funded and pending, and what is left, in pre-discount dollars, plus the entity's own certified FY2026 and FY2027 Form 470s. Consortia have no C2 budget of their own.

Sources: USAC Open Data 6brt-5pbv, USAC Open Data jt8s-3q52. License of the source data: Public Domain (the license USAC Open Data gives the source datasets). Page: E-Rate C2 Budget Lookup, FY2026–2030 (Category 2).

E-Rate FCC Form 470 bid requests by state, FY2027
Certified FCC Form 470s for funding year 2027, one row per form (its latest version), by the billed entity's state, with Category 1 and Category 2 requests and forms still in their bid period.

Sources: USAC Open Data jp7a-89nd, USAC Open Data jt8s-3q52, USAC Open Data qdmp-ygft. License of the source data: Public Domain (the license USAC Open Data gives the source datasets). Page: E-Rate Form 470 Bid Requests by State, FY2027.

E-Rate FCC Form 470s certified by October 31: FY2027 vs FY2026 and FY2024
Distinct Form 470 application numbers certified from July 1 to October 31 of the year each funding year's window opened (2026 for FY2027, 2025 for FY2026, 2023 for FY2024), with status Certified today.

Sources: USAC Open Data jp7a-89nd, USAC Open Data 39tn-hjzv, USAC Open Data 6brt-5pbv. License of the source data: Public Domain (the license USAC Open Data gives the source datasets). The page has its own how to cite note. Page: FY2027 E-Rate Form 470s vs FY2026, July–October.

California pending on-premise liquor license applications by county
Pending on-premise applications (restaurants, bars, hotels, clubs) in California ABC's public daily file, by premises county. ABC's file has no filing date, so "new" means a file number that wasn't in an earlier daily snapshot of ours.

Sources: California ABC licensing reports. License of the source data: California ABC Conditions of Use: information on its website "is considered in the public domain". Page: California Pending Liquor License Applications by County.

NYC Local Law 11 (FISP) sub-cycle 10A filing status, by borough
Sub-cycle 10A facades on the NYC Department of Buildings' FISP list by filing status and borough. Sub-cycle 10A covers buildings taller than six stories on tax blocks ending in 4, 5, 6 or 9. Counts only: no addresses, owners or inspectors.

Sources: NYC DOB FISP list, NYC Open Data xubg-57si. The page has its own how to cite note. Page: NYC Local Law 11 (FISP) Sub-Cycle 10A Filing Status.

NOAA 1991–2020 normal weather days by month at 10 large US metro stations
NOAA 1991–2020 monthly normals at each metro's main first-order station: days with 0.10 inch or more of precipitation, days with a low at or below 32 °F and days with a high at or above 90 °F.

Sources: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals. License of the source data: Public domain: NOAA NCEI data are U.S. government works (17 U.S.C. 105). Page: Weather Delay Days vs. NOAA Normals · Westworks.
