How to read this

  • Contracts allowed from is USAC's allowable contract date: the earliest day the applicant may sign, usually 28 days after the form was certified. Bid due dates in the RFP can be later.
  • Most recent C2 vendor appears when the applicant has no FY2026 Category 2 request: it is the provider on its latest funded C2 requests in FY2021–25 (a new five-year C2 budget cycle began in FY2026). Quantities are as the applicant entered them; some look like placeholders (for example, 24 ft of cabling).
  • Incumbent = the service provider on the applicant's own FY2026 funding requests (FRNs) in the same category, funded or still pending. The figures in brackets come from those FRNs: bids is the number of bids the applicant reported for the contract behind the FRN (for multi-year contracts that procurement can predate FY2026); ends is the contract expiration date (for month-to-month and tariff services USAC shows the service end date); $/mo is the pre-discount monthly recurring cost; Category 2 rows with no recurring cost show the FRN's pre-discount total instead.
  • C2 budget left = USAC's available C2 budget for the FY2026–30 cycle: budget minus funded minus pending, pre-discount.
  • "None on file" usually means a first-time or returning applicant, or one served through a consortium's or another entity's funding requests, which this BEN-level match does not follow.
