Request a refund

We refund a brief in full:

  • (a) if it misstates a date, time, quantity, score or requirement the RFP states, or leaves out a stated disqualifier, and you ask before the RFP's due date or within 30 days of delivery, whichever is later;
  • (b) for any reason, if you ask within 7 days of delivery;
  • (c) automatically, without being asked, if we don't deliver within 24 hours of payment, and we still deliver;
  • (d) at once, if the Form 470 has no readable RFP on USAC (for example, its files only point to the applicant's own portal, or the RFP is only scanned images), isn't a certified FY2027 form, or its RFP due date has passed.

For an order of several briefs, refunds are per brief: a brief's refund is its share of what you paid, tax included. Link may also process refunds under its own policies. Otherwise our Terms (section 18) apply.
