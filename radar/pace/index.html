FY2027 E-Rate Form 470s are running 40% behind last year

1,820 FCC Form 470s were certified for E-Rate funding year 2027 from July 1 to October 31, 2026. By the same date a year earlier, FY2026 had 3,025 (−39.8%). By the same date in 2023, FY2024 had 2,025 (−10.1%).

Category 2 budgets not yet requested

Of the $12.40 billion in FY2026–2030 Category 2 budgets for 32,956 budgeted entities, $10.02 billion (80.8%) is not yet requested: $1.58 billion is funded and $0.80 billion pending. These are pre-discount dollars; budgets still marked Forecast can change. Look up any entity in the free C2 budget lookup.

Source: USAC Open Data, E-Rate C2 Budget Tool Data FY2021+ (6brt-5pbv), cycle FY2026-2030, as of USAC's Nov 5, 2026 update.

What the data can't say

  • Why. The counts don't explain the drop. The gap was already there before October: 525 forms vs 931 from July to September.
  • Early season. Most forms come from November to February, so a slow fall need not mean a smaller season.
  • Forms, not dollars. One form can cover many entities and services, so these are counts of bid requests, not demand or spending.
  • Category 2 and the budget cycle. FY2026 opened a new five-year Category 2 budget cycle, so FY2026 is a cycle-start year. Against FY2024, Category 2 requests are up (919 vs 764, +20.3%).
  • Late rows. USAC updates daily, and a day's late rows can add a handful of forms. A few FY2027 forms will still be canceled.

Method

  • Distinct FCC Form 470 application numbers (Original rows) in E-Rate Open Competitive Bidding: Basic Information (FCC Form 470 and Related Information) (jp7a-89nd), certified on or before October 31 of the year each window opened (2026 for FY2027, 2025 for FY2026, 2023 for FY2024), with status Certified today. The "ever certified" row adds forms certified by then and canceled since.
  • A form requests Category 1 or 2 when any of its service lines in E-Rate Open Competitive Bidding: Services Requested (FCC Form 470 and Related Information) (39tn-hjzv) carries that category.
  • Category 2 budgets: 6brt-5pbv, cycle FY2026-2030; "not yet requested" is USAC's available amount (budget minus funded minus pending).
  • Queries (endpoint https://opendata.usac.org/resource/<id>.json; the other years swap the year and the date):
jp7a-89nd: $select=count(distinct application_number)&$where=funding_year='2027' AND form_version='Original' AND f470_status='Certified' AND certified_datetime <= '2026-10-31T23:59:59.999'

jp7a-89nd: $select=funding_year, f470_status, date_trunc_ym(certified_datetime) as m, count(distinct application_number) as forms&$where=funding_year in('2024','2025','2026','2027') AND form_version='Original'&$group=funding_year, f470_status, m

39tn-hjzv: $select=application_number, service_category, function, form_version, count(*)&$where=funding_year='2027'&$group=application_number, service_category, function, form_version

6brt-5pbv: $select=state, count(*), sum(c2_budget), sum(funded_c2_budget_amount), sum(pending_c2_budget_amount), sum(available_c2_budget_amount)&$where=c2_budget_cycle='FY2026-2030'&$group=state
