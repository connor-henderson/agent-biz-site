What you get

  • Every new Form 470 in your state(s), filtered to your categories:
    • Category 1: internet access, transport, leased fiber.
    • Category 2: Wi-Fi, switching, cabling, firewalls, UPS, racks, maintenance, managed Wi-Fi.
  • For each form: the applicant, its type and size; the Form 470 number, linked to the official USAC record; the certification date and the allowable contract date; the services and quantities requested, with any brand preference; whether an RFP is attached, and whether the applicant flags state or local procurement rules.
  • The overlay, from the applicant's own funding requests:
    • Category 1: the FY2026 incumbent.
    • Category 2: the FY2026 incumbent, or else the most recent funded vendor from FY2021–25.
    • Plus the bids received, the contract end date, the monthly cost (C1) or pre-discount total (C2), and the remaining FY2026–30 Category 2 budget.
  • Each week: the districts in your state with the most unspent Category 2 budget, and whether each has posted a Form 470 yet.
  • The same rows as plain-text CSV inside the email, ready to paste into a spreadsheet. (File attachments come later, when our mail desk supports them.)
  • Your first digest also lists every FY2027 form in your state that is still inside its bid period.
