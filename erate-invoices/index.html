If the deadline has passed

USAC's Invoice Deadline Extensions page gives two routes, depending on how long ago the last date to invoice (USAC's "IDD") was:

  • Within 15 days after it: "If your IDD has passed but you are still within 15 days of the IDD, you can request a one-time invoice deadline extension by opening a Customer Service Case in EPC and listing the FRN(s) you want to extend." USAC calls this "an interim process that will remain in place until EPC can handle these requests directly." On who can ask, the same page says: "To request an extension in EPC, you must have full or partial rights to Invoicing." It places those rights on the billed entity's EPC profile for an applicant, on the profile of the BEN on the Form 471 for a consultant, and on the SPIN's profile for a service provider.
  • More than 15 days after it: "If you need an invoice deadline extension, but the deadline to request one has passed (i.e., it is more than 15 days after the IDD), you must first submit a waiver request to the Federal Communications Commission (FCC)."

Quotes from USAC's page as read on November 4, 2026. This page counts the 15 days as calendar days; USAC's page doesn't say calendar or business days. Information only, not E-Rate compliance advice: confirm the route, the dates and your invoicing rights in EPC before you act.

How to read a result

No invoice received yet
USAC's FRN Status data shows the invoicing mode as "NOT SET", which USAC defines as no invoice form received yet. Under BEAR (FCC Form 472) the applicant pays its provider in full and files for reimbursement; under SPI (FCC Form 474) the provider bills the applicant only its share and invoices USAC for the rest.
Committed
The dollars USAC committed to the FRN (its share after the discount). It is the most that can be invoiced, not a promise that the full amount is owed.
Last date to invoice
USAC's "last date an invoice (BEAR or SPI) can be received or postmarked". USAC sets it at the latest of 120 days after the last day to receive service, 120 days after the funding commitment decision letter, or 120 days after a revised one.
Extension on file
Each FRN gets one automatic 120-day invoice deadline extension if it is requested in EPC before, or within 15 days after, the deadline. "Yes" means USAC's data shows an extension request for the FRN, so the date shown has most likely been extended once already. Past the 15-day window, USAC says the FCC must first grant a waiver.
Not ready for invoicing
USAC's "invoicing ready" flag is No. That usually means a step before invoicing (such as the Form 486) isn't complete in USAC's records.
