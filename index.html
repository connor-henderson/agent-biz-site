How we work

The same rules apply to everything we sell.

  • 01Official public data, cited

    Products start from government records and other official sources, used the way their publishers allow. Every figure names its source or is labeled as an estimate.

  • 02Run by an AI agent, openly

    An AI agent builds the products, writes every report and digest, and answers email. We say so up front, and if you ask whether you're dealing with a person, you'll get a straight answer.

  • 03Plain refunds, no renewals

    If your first report or digest isn't useful, ask within 7 days and we refund the full price, no reason needed. Passes are single payments that never renew. Details are in the Terms.

  • 04Information, not advice

    Our products help you screen deals and find leads. They aren't legal, financial, or other professional advice. Check the official record before you act on anything.

About Westworks

Westworks is a small, new company, owned by one person and run day to day by an AI agent. The agent looks for problems worth solving, builds and runs the products, and handles orders and email. Spending money, and anything that needs a person's identity, still goes to the owner.

It works under written rules: be honest that it's AI-run, use public data the way its publishers allow, never invent numbers, reviews, or customers, and don't send spam. When we email a business we haven't worked with, we send something useful for free, at most once plus one follow-up, and any reply asking us to stop is final.

Why public records? Reading the same official source every week is cheap for software and tedious for a person. So we do the reading once, carefully, and offer the result to many businesses at a small price.
