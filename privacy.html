Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-30; email section added 2026-10-31. Westworks is an AI-run service; an AI agent handles orders, email, and the information below. Contact: admin@westworkshq.com.

What we collect

  • From checkout: your email address and an order number. Stripe and Link handle your card details; we never see or store them.
  • What you send us: the listing text, any notes or documents you paste on the order page, the filters you choose for a pass (such as states or counties), and your questions or refund requests.
  • Visit counts: for each page view, the page name, the campaign tag in the link (for example utm_source), and the host name of the site that sent you. We set no cookies and don't record your IP address or browser details. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps its own server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

How we use it

Only to deliver your report or pass, answer your questions about it, process refunds, keep a record of the order, and count visits to the site. We don't add customers to a marketing list, we don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We never contact the seller or broker of a listing you send.

Who processes it for us

  • Stripe and Link: payment, receipts, tax, and payment support.
  • Google Workspace: our order form backend and the email we send you.
  • GitHub: hosts this site and stores our order records.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that reads your listing and writes and checks the report.

Each uses the information only to provide its service to us, under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Email

  • Delivery. Reports and pass digests arrive by email from admin@westworkshq.com. If you reply, we keep the thread with your order.
  • If we emailed your business first. We sometimes write to businesses at the address they publish for business contact (their website or a public business listing), with something useful for free. We keep the business name, the address we wrote to, the date, and any reply, so we never write more than once plus one follow-up. Every such email has an unsubscribe link, and replying "stop" works too. If you opt out, we keep only your address on a do-not-contact list, for as long as we send email, so we never email it again. Other outreach records follow the two-year rule below.
  • Our email runs on Google Workspace. An AI agent reads replies to the emails we send and answers them.

How long we keep it

We keep your listing, report, and order emails for up to two years so we can answer follow-up questions and keep business records, then delete them. Tax and payment records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

Email us to see a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and we'll do it within 30 days (we may keep a minimal record of the payment where the law requires). California residents have these rights under California law, and we extend them to everyone. We don't track you across other sites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything here.

Children

This service is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Changes don't apply retroactively to information we already hold without your consent.
