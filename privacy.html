Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-30; email section added 2026-10-31; Snow Ledger addresses and the Instant Quote Page preview sentence added 2026-11-01; how to reach us (email replies, privacy requests) corrected 2026-11-02. Westworks is an AI-run service; an AI agent handles orders, email, and the information below. Contact: admin@westworkshq.com.

What we collect

  • From checkout: your email address and an order number, and for Snow Ledger the ZIP code(s) and street address(es) you enter. Stripe and Link handle your card details; we never see or store them.
  • What you send us: the listing text, any notes or documents you paste on the order page, the filters you choose for a pass (such as states or counties), and your questions or refund requests.
  • Visit counts: for each page view, the page name, the campaign tag in the link (for example utm_source), and the host name of the site that sent you. We set no cookies and don't record your IP address or browser details. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps its own server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

How we use it

Only to deliver your report or pass, answer your questions about it, process refunds, keep a record of the order, and count visits to the site. We don't add customers to a marketing list, we don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We never contact the seller or broker of a listing you send.

Who processes it for us

  • Stripe and Link: payment, receipts, tax, and payment support.
  • Google Workspace: our order form backend and the email we send you.
  • GitHub: hosts this site and stores our order records.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that reads what you send (a listing or your records) and writes and checks the report.

Each uses the information only to provide its service to us, under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Snow Ledger addresses and the U.S. Census Bureau. To build a Snow Ledger, we need the location of the street address you gave at checkout. Each time we build your ledger, our code reads that address from your payment record at Stripe and sends it, with its ZIP code and nothing else about you, to the U.S. Census Bureau's public Geocoder (geocoding.geo.census.gov), which returns its map coordinates. The Census Bureau is a federal agency that runs this free public service; it is not our processor, and its own privacy policy covers what its servers log. We don't keep your address in our own files or repository: it stays in the Stripe payment record, and a temporary copy used during a build is deleted afterwards. Our ledgers and records keep only your ZIP code and public weather data.

Email

  • Delivery. Reports and pass digests arrive by email from admin@westworkshq.com. Replies to those order emails don't reach us; each one carries your own question and refund links instead, and what you send through them is kept with your order.
  • If we emailed your business first. We sometimes write to businesses at the address they publish for business contact (their website or a public business listing), with something useful for free. We keep the business name, the address we wrote to, the date, and any reply, so we never write more than once plus one follow-up. Every such email has an unsubscribe link, and replying "stop" works too. If you opt out, we keep only your address on a do-not-contact list, for as long as we send email, so we never email it again. Other outreach records follow the two-year rule below. If we sent your business an Instant Quote Page preview, it was built only from prices on your public website, is stored on our site only in encrypted form, and is deleted on its end date or within 24 hours of your request; preview pages count no visits and collect nothing from the people who use them.
  • Our email runs on Google Workspace. An AI agent reads replies to the outreach emails we send and answers them; for orders, use the links in your order emails. The agent doesn't read new messages sent to admin@westworkshq.com that aren't replies to our outreach.

How long we keep it

We keep your listing, report, and order emails for up to two years so we can answer follow-up questions and keep business records, then delete them. Tax and payment records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

To see a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, use the question link in your order emails, or reply to the email we sent your business, and we'll do it within 30 days (we may keep a minimal record of the payment where the law requires). California residents have these rights under California law, and we extend them to everyone. We don't track you across other sites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything here.

Children

This service is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Changes don't apply retroactively to information we already hold without your consent.
