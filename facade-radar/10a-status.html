Local Law 11: 4,880 of 6,632 sub-cycle 10A facades show no report filed

The NYC Department of Buildings' own FISP list (file last modified November 4, 2026) shows 4,880 of the 6,632 sub-cycle 10A facades with “No Report Filed” (74%). Sub-cycle 10A covers buildings taller than six stories on tax blocks ending in 4, 5, 6 or 9, and their Cycle 10 facade reports are due by February 21, 2027.

The deadline and the penalties

  • DOB's Cycle 10 service notice: “4, 5, 6, 9 (Sub Cycle A) … February 21, 2025 – February 21, 2027” (service notice).
  • DOB's fees page: “Penalties apply after the filing period ends. Late Filing (initial report) $1,000 per month. Failure to File (initial report) $5,000 per year” (fees and penalties; rule text: 1 RCNY §103-04).
  • The latest rule change covers penalty waivers only. DOB's amendments to 1 RCNY §103-04 (public hearing December 8, 2025) were adopted, effective February 1, 2026 (NYC Rules). Waiver requests must now be made “through the Department's website, submitted with the filing fee” ($140 on the fees page), and a demolition condition was added. The amendments don't change the cycle or the filing windows: the rule still sets five-year cycles and ends sub-cycle A's window on “February 21 of years ending in two (2) and seven (7)”.
  • A longer cycle is in the law, not yet in DOB's rule. Local Law 49 of 2025, in effect since October 1, 2026, says the examinations happen “at periodic intervals as set forth by rule of the commissioner”, “provided such periodic intervals are between 6 to 12 years”. On March 6, 2026 the Mayor's office said DOB will “extend the timeline for inspections an extra year, from five to six years” (announcement). DOB hasn't proposed that rule: its FY2027 regulatory agenda lists a facade rule for the second half of the fiscal year (January to June 2027), and none is among the proposed rules. The current rule and DOB's Cycle 10 service notice and diagram still give sub-cycle 10A a February 21, 2027 deadline.

Rule status checked November 4, 2026: NYC Rules (rules.cityofnewyork.us), DOB's rule PDF, fees page, Cycle 10 service notice and diagram, and Local Law 49 of 2025.

What the data can't say

  • Upper bound. “No Report Filed” can include reports filed but not yet accepted, for example while owed penalties are unpaid, and early filers can use a sub-cycle override.
  • Coverage. DOB's list “is based on City records. It is ultimately the building owner’s responsibility to correctly identify whether their building is subject to the Façades Inspection and Safety Program” (FISP page).
  • Two counts, two units. DOB's list counts facade control numbers (6,632 facades on 6,557 buildings, BINs). Our Open Data recount counts BINs on the Cycle 9 sub-cycle A roster (6,306). Matched by BIN, 4,401 buildings are unfiled on both; 165 of our 4,566 aren't on DOB's 10A list, and 142 of DOB's 4,834 “No Report Filed” BINs show a Cycle 10 filing in Open Data (likely in review).

Method

  • DOB's FISP list: the downloadable list linked from the FISP Universe Map, which DOB links from its FISP page; 73,131 rows in the file of November 4, 2026. Only four columns are read (Cycle, Current Filing Status, Borough, Bin), and only counts are kept: the file also carries personal names, which we never store or show.
  • NYC Open Data recount: DOB NOW: Safety – Facades Compliance Filings (xubg-57si), as of the dataset's November 2, 2026 update. The cohort is every BIN with a Cycle 9 report tagged 9A; a building counts as filed once it has any Cycle 10 row that isn't Auto-Generated. DOB test records are dropped. One pull:
https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/xubg-57si.json?$select=tr6_no, filing_type, cycle, bin, borough, block, sequence_no, filing_date, filing_status, current_status&$where=cycle in('9','10')

NYC Open Data FAQ: “There are no restrictions on the use of Open Data.” A building-level list (a free sample here, the full list for subscribers) is on the NYC Facade Radar sample page. To check one building, use DOB's FISP map.
