What you get each week

  • New UNSAFE and SWARMP reports, Cycle 10, as DOB posts them. Many reports reach DOB's public data days or weeks after their filing date, so each issue carries every one posted since the last issue, whatever its filing date. For each one: the filing date, the rating at filing and now, the prior-cycle rating; the address, borough, ZIP and stories; BIN, block, lot and TR6 number; the QEWI firm that signed it; the owner or managing agent when the filing names a business; the wall type and material; and a link to the official record.
  • Sidewalk-shed status. Whether a sidewalk-shed permit is on file for the same building (DOB NOW: Build). Most UNSAFE buildings already have one: in October, 41 of 48 did before the report was filed, and 5 had none in the last 12 months. We flag those.
  • The 10A deadline list. Every building on DOB's sub-cycle 9A roster with no Cycle 10 report on file (4,566 as of November 1, 2026), with its last Cycle 9 rating and date, its Cycle 9 QEWI firm, the owner or agent where it's a business, and a flag when another building on the same tax lot has filed.
  • Who dropped off. The 10A buildings whose first Cycle 10 report was posted since the last issue (164 in October).
  • Your areas first. Your boroughs and ZIP codes lead each list; the full citywide list comes as an attached file you can open in any browser and paste into a spreadsheet.

Why now

Sub-cycle 10A (blocks ending in 4, 5, 6 or 9) must file by February 21, 2027 (DOB Cycle 10 service notice, February 24, 2025). DOB's penalty for a late initial report is $1,000 a month (DOB FISP fees and penalties, checked November 1, 2026). Last cycle, 47% of sub-cycle 9A's initial reports came in during the final 112 days before the deadline, and 34% after it (same dataset; Cycle 9 overlapped COVID). The pass covers the crunch and the first weeks of late filings, and ends on March 31, 2027. Nothing to cancel.
