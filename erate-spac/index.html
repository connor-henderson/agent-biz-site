FY2026 E-Rate SPAC: funded requests whose provider shows no Form 473 on file

1,223 funded FY2026 E-Rate requests (FRNs), with $46.7 million committed, are with service providers that USAC's data shows with no FY2026 SPAC (FCC Form 473, the Service Provider Annual Certification) on file. That is 2.5% of the 48,521 funded FY2026 requests and 2.0% of their committed dollars.

The other 47,298 of the 48,521 funded FY2026 requests are with providers whose FY2026 SPAC is on file.

USAC's rule, in its own words

From USAC's File the FCC Form 473 page:

  • “Service providers must file their SPAC Form before any FCC Forms 472 (BEAR) or FCC Forms 474 (SPI) invoices can be processed and paid by USAC.”
  • “USAC must have a SPAC on file for the relevant funding year in order to be able to disburse funds for an FRN for that funding year.”
  • “FCC rules require service providers to annually submit a SPAC Form for each Service Provider Identification Number (SPIN)/498 ID for every funding year that they participate in the E-Rate program.”
  • “File this form in the E-Rate Productivity Center (EPC).”
  • “View the status of your SPAC for a particular funding year in the Service Provider (SPIN) Download Tool or the Open Data FRN Status Tool FY2016 to verify that USAC has successfully processed your SPAC form.”

From USAC's FCC Form 472 (BEAR Form) page: “The FCC Form 472, or BEAR Form can be filed after:” … “The service provider has filed an FCC Form 473 (Service Provider Annual Certification (SPAC) Form) for that funding year.”

From USAC's E-Rate announcement of June 18, 2026, FY2026 Invoicing Begins on July 1: “Service provider(s) must have a FY2026 FCC Form 473 (SPAC) on file before any invoices can be processed or funding disbursed.”

So, by USAC's own rule, USAC can't disburse on any of these requests until the provider's FY2026 SPAC is on file, and for a request set to BEAR invoicing, USAC's BEAR page lists the provider's filed Form 473 among the conditions for filing the BEAR.

Quoted word for word from USAC's pages as read on November 10, 2026. This page adds no advice of its own: USAC's pages and EPC are the authority.

What the data can't say

  • Committed but not disbursed isn't money owed. A service may not be delivered yet, an invoice may not be filed, and other conditions apply.
  • A SPAC is necessary, not sufficient. USAC also needs the applicant's approved FCC Form 486 (the ready-to-invoice flag above). For a BEAR, USAC's BEAR page also lists “The applicant has paid their service provider in full for the eligible equipment and services received.” And since September 8, 2026, USAC pays through SAM.gov (SAM.gov UEI Requirement); its announcement of September 17, 2026 says: “SPI and BEAR filers who have not completed the SAM.gov registration process and added their UEI to EPC can submit invoices but will not receive disbursements until these steps are completed.” So this data can't say how much filing a SPAC alone would release.
  • USAC's processed status, which can trail EPC. The flag is what USAC shows once it has processed a SPAC: its Form 473 page points providers to this data “to verify that USAC has successfully processed your SPAC form”. So a SPAC filed recently may not show yet. EPC shows the current status.
  • The data doesn't say why. USAC's data records whether a SPAC is on file, not why one isn't.
  • SPINs, not companies. 570 is USAC's count of service provider numbers; a company can hold more than one SPIN, and the count includes USAC's interim placeholder SPIN, which is not a company.
  • One day's data. USAC updates its data daily, and these counts change as SPACs are processed. This page is one update, dated above.

By invoicing mode

Each FY2026 request is set to one of two ways of invoicing USAC. BEAR (FCC Form 472), in USAC's words: “Applicants file an FCC Form 472 (Billed Entity Applicant Reimbursement (BEAR) Form) to request reimbursement from USAC for the discounted cost of eligible equipment and services approved on the FCC Form 471, and which the applicant has received and paid in full to its service provider.” Under SPI (FCC Form 474), the provider bills the applicant its share and invoices USAC for the rest.

BEAR or SPI here is how each request is set to be invoiced, not an invoice that was filed: every FY2026 request has a mode set before any invoice is filed (USAC's column note says the third value, NOT SET, is “only applicable for funding years before 2026”). Ready to invoice is USAC's invoicing-ready flag: “Invoicing may begin for the FRN as there is an approved FCC Form 486.” A SPIN can have requests in both modes (62 do), so the two SPIN counts add up to more than 570.

Method and sources

  • The set. USAC Open Data, E-Rate Request for Discount on Services: FRN Status (FCC Form 471 and Related Information) (qdmp-ygft, licensed Public Domain; rows updated Nov 10, 2026, 10:37 UTC): FY2026 requests, form version Current, status Funded, committed above $0, whose SPAC Filed? value is not Yes. USAC's column note: “Indicates if an FCC Form 473 Service Provider Annual Certification (SPAC) was filed for the Funding Year associated with the FCC Form 471.”
  • The counts. Requests are distinct FRNs. Committed is USAC's funding commitment (“The total amount for the Funding Request (FRN) after discount.”), summed. SPINs are distinct service provider numbers; applicants are distinct billed entity numbers (BENs); the state is the applicant's. Disbursed is USAC's total authorized disbursement (“Total net amount disbursed to a service provider or applicant.”).
  • Small groups. A row with fewer than 3 SPINs or fewer than 3 applicants is combined with others into one row, so no row points at a single provider or applicant. A combined row holds at least two groups and itself has at least 3 SPINs and 3 applicants; if needed, the next smallest row joins it.
  • Checks. USAC's server recounted the national and per-state figures (requests, committed, SPINs, applicants) and agreed with the counts made from the rows. USAC's second source, E-Rate Service Provider Information (xcy2-bdid, rows updated Nov 10, 2026, 10:30 UTC; its metadata names no license, so this page takes one count from it), lists a FY2026 Form 473 for none of these 570 SPINs. Its column note: “Lists the funding years for which the service provider has filed an FCC Form 473.”
  • Queries (endpoint https://opendata.usac.org/resource/<dataset>.json), pulled Nov 10, 2026, 14:59 UTC:
qdmp-ygft: $select=funding_year,form_version,form_471_frn_status_name,funding_request_number,epc_organization_id,ben,state,organization_entity_type_name,form_471_service_type_name,funding_commitment_request,total_authorized_disbursement,invoicing_mode,invoicing_ready,spac_filed&$where=funding_year='2026' AND form_version='Current' AND form_471_frn_status_name='Funded' AND funding_commitment_request > 0 AND (spac_filed IS NULL OR spac_filed != 'Yes')&$order=funding_request_number

qdmp-ygft: $select=spac_filed, invoicing_mode, invoicing_ready, count(*) as rows, count(distinct funding_request_number) as frns, sum(funding_commitment_request) as committed&$where=funding_year='2026' AND form_version='Current' AND form_471_frn_status_name='Funded' AND funding_commitment_request > 0&$group=spac_filed, invoicing_mode, invoicing_ready&$order=spac_filed, invoicing_mode, invoicing_ready

qdmp-ygft: $select=count(distinct funding_request_number) as frns, sum(funding_commitment_request) as committed, count(distinct epc_organization_id) as spins, count(distinct ben) as applicants, count(*) as rows, count(distinct state) as states, count(total_authorized_disbursement) as with_value&$where=funding_year='2026' AND form_version='Current' AND form_471_frn_status_name='Funded' AND funding_commitment_request > 0 AND (spac_filed IS NULL OR spac_filed != 'Yes')

qdmp-ygft: $select=state, count(distinct funding_request_number) as frns, sum(funding_commitment_request) as committed, count(distinct epc_organization_id) as spins, count(distinct ben) as applicants&$where=funding_year='2026' AND form_version='Current' AND form_471_frn_status_name='Funded' AND funding_commitment_request > 0 AND (spac_filed IS NULL OR spac_filed != 'Yes')&$group=state&$order=state

xcy2-bdid: $select=spin, fcc_form_473_spac_filed&$where=fcc_form_473_spac_filed like '%2026%'&$order=spin
